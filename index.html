Coffeebreak Community Food Hampers

Free, charity-based food hampers for people doing it tough in Newcastle and the Hunter. No cost, no judgement, just neighbours looking after neighbours.

Request a free hamper

Hampers are free and depend on donations and what we have available in the Newcastle and Hunter area, so we cannot promise a hamper or a delivery time. This is not an emergency service. If you need food today, please use the services in Get help now. In an emergency call 000.


    

    
Sending opens your email app with the request filled in, addressed to songwriterkaz@gmail.com. If it does not open, tap Copy request and paste it into an email to that address. Your details are only used to arrange your hamper.

Donate food or funds

    
Every can, packet and dollar goes into hampers for local families and individuals. Donations of food can be arranged by email to songwriterkaz@gmail.com.

    
Pay by PayID (Teachers Mutual Bank): darkokaz@gmx.com

    
Please add "Hamper donation" as the payment description. Coffeebreak Community Food Hampers is a community effort and is not a registered charity, so donations are not tax deductible.
